# ⛵ Boat Battle

Play it at https://verdeyen-k.github.io/boatbattle/

2 to 8 boats (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink, teal) sail around one sea and fire their cannons at each other.
Every boat has 3 lives. Last boat floating wins!

## Power-ups

Crates float up on the water. Sail into one to load a special shot. It replaces your cannon until its shots run out:

| | Power-up | Shots | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⛓️ | Double barrel | 5 | Two cannonballs chained together, so it's hard to miss |
| 🦔 | Spiky | 1 | Does 2 damage |
| 💥 | Boom | 3 | Explodes and hits every boat nearby |

The plain cannon never runs out.

## Controls

- **Phone or tablet:** push the stick the way you want to sail, and hold FIRE.
- **Keyboard:** arrow keys or WASD to steer, Space to fire.

## Ways to play

- **Practice:** you against 1 to 7 computer boats. Pick how good they are: Very Easy, Easy, Medium, Hard or Insane.
- **Online:** one person creates a room, and everyone else types the 4-letter code on their own device.

Made with plain HTML and JavaScript. Online rooms use Firebase Realtime Database.
